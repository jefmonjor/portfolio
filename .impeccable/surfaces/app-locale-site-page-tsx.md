# Home (/, /es)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: owners, ops leads and CTOs of 10–200 person businesses with a problem software can solve. Action: open the project brief ("Let's discuss your project"). Proof: seven years across banking, legal, energy, telecom; live products Transolido, Other Twist, Ilnami; the anonymised bank case; his own portrait. Constraints: no invented clients, figures or screenshots; static, works without JS, reduced motion respected, self-hosted images with Unsplash credits.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Software as architecture. The home reads like an architecture studio's monograph: load-bearing structures photographed, the software systems drawn over them in amber linework. It refuses the SaaS hero (centred headline, button, row of equal cards) and the stat-band template.

OWN-WORLD: Stone-white ground and ink type; black-and-white architectural photography as the major surfaces, framed with 24px corners inside the gutters; burnt amber only where something carries load (the italic accent word, signals travelling on lines, the CTA arrow, the closing steel); Instrument Serif display with italic accents over Inter text; hairline linework that draws and carries pulses.

STORY: The visitor meets a brutalist building and "Software built like architecture.", reads the offer in one line, sees where the experience comes from, follows how disconnected tools become one system, opens live cases, picks a way to start, meets the architect, and closes on amber steel at night with the brief.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A framed photograph about 86svh tall across the page; headline bottom-left at up to 6rem in white with "architecture" in amber italic; the subline beneath; a light primary button and a text link to the work; the availability badge above the headline; amber linework tracing the facade with pulses; the photo credit in small type bottom-right.

FORM: pinned by the owner (editorial architecture-studio references), position 1 of 1. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
