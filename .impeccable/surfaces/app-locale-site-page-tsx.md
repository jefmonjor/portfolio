# Home (/, /es)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: owners, ops leads and CTOs of 10–200 person businesses with a problem software can solve. Action: open the project brief ("Let's discuss your project"). Proof: an animated orbit of the real tools he builds with; three live products he built and runs (Transolido, Other Twist, Ilnami) shown as real screenshots of their public pages; seven years across banking, legal, energy and telecom; the anonymised bank case. Constraints: no invented clients, testimonials, figures or prices; static, works without JS, reduced motion respected, self-hosted images.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A person, not a studio. The page reads like the senior consultants the owner pinned (happycoders.eu, vladmihalcea.com/consulting, alexedwards.net/work-with-me): first person, what he solves and for whom, named services a CTO can buy, real proof, one call to action. It refuses the AI-landing clusters (cream ground, italic serif accent, terracotta; dark hero with glowing annotations), stock photography and a studio look that hides the trade.

OWN-WORLD: White ground with neutral cool greys and ink; one committed pine green (#0F6B4F light, #5FD1A0 dark) for the primary button, the "I decide" review gate and the closing field; Schibsted Grotesk display at 600–650 with tight tracking over Inter text; 24px portrait and panel corners; real screenshots in a minimal browser frame showing the live domain.

STORY: The visitor reads three outcomes, meets Jefferson by name, sees every tool he builds with orbiting "your system" and three products live in production, follows how scattered tools become one system, learns how he works with AI under senior review, checks the work, picks a service by "best for / you get / format", reads a short first-person note and closes on a green panel with the brief.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Two columns from 64rem. Left: availability badge, the headline as three sentences on three lines (up to 3.9rem), a first-person subline, the green primary button with "See my work" beside it, and "You talk to the engineer who builds it". Right (26rem): the tools orbit — "Your system" on a green disc at the centre, 24 real tool logos on three slowly counter-rotating rings, a green signal travelling each ring — and below it the white "Live in production" card with links to the three products. On phones the orbit follows the copy. The owner's photo never appears on the site; it lives only on /cv.

FORM: canon, pinned by the owner (personal senior-consultant sites), position 1 of 1. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
