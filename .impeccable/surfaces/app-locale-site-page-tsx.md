# Home (/, /es)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: CTOs, founders and technical leads with something to build, modernize or put AI into, and recruiters filling a senior role (versatile profile, not a sector). Action: "Let's talk" (project brief) or, for recruiters, the full CV at /cv. Proof: an anonymised bank case, six products live in production shown as real captures of their public pages, the reviewed-AI pipeline and the tools orbit; seven years across banking, legal, energy and telecom. Constraints: no invented clients, logos, testimonials, figures or prices; no cream ground; no owner photo; static, works without JS, reduced motion respected, self-hosted images.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The work is the page. One sentence (the systems companies run on, the products on top of them and the AI that works inside both), then two columns: the systems on the left, the products on the right, with the AI proven inside both. It refuses the long sectioned landing (hero, features, process, about, closing) and the studio look that hides the trade: half the wall is engineering artefacts, not screenshots.

OWN-WORLD: Cool grey ground with white 16px panels and no rings; ink text; pine green only on the one action, the review gate, the orbit's core and the closing field; Bricolage Grotesque 700 at -0.04em for the headline, Source Sans 3 for running text; real captures labelled with their live domain; a fixed 18rem rail carrying name, role, navigation, the action, availability and, with room, the assistant docked at its foot.

STORY: The visitor reads what he builds; on the left sees the bank case, the tools, the reviewed-AI pipeline, the three ways to work and Jefferson by name with the bank figures; on the right six live products, the note for recruiters and the brief. Phones read the proof first: bank, products, then the method.

FIRST VIEWPORT: From 75rem, the rail on the left: name, role, six links, the green "Let's talk", availability with a still dot, language, terminal and theme, "I have worked for" and the assistant. Main: the headline up to 2.875rem on three lines in English and four in Spanish, a two-sentence subline, and at about two fifths of the viewport the wall begins, bank case left, told in words, and the Transolido sign-in right. Below 75rem: top bar, headline, subline, action, availability, one column.

FORM: reference pinned by the owner (geronimo.design: rail, work wall, short page), translated to the brand and adopted after an independent finish review; position 1 of 1. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
